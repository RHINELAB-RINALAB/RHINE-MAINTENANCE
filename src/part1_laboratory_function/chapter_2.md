# Member, personnel and logistics

As far as it is concerned, we intend to keep the core of operation for the laboratory to be low. That is, deliberately so, we are to reduce the operational cost in general to the baseline minimum possible so to prevent ourselves from instead of doing academic works, into managing administrative bloat, unnecessary bureaucratic performance that does not serve to clarify or of any given strict design. 

Thereby, the current decision is thus configured for *limited core functional*, that is, the core full member and official members of RHINELAB, would be, and happens of, a small and dedicated slot only, of which is approved by the Laboratory Leader. Given such, the current size is determined mostly of $X$ people, and they would be divided into tiers and slot of what is available and so on. Insurance for this is mostly there for minimal core of actionable population, for example, the director of logistics and so on (which is responsibility role). 

> [!IMPORTANT] 
>  Under current scheme of logistical support and management, we cap the total amount of official members to **30 members** only. We apologize for this small cohort choice. 

Logistics are handled in the logistical section. However, here, we are to also deliver the assignment on logistical maintenance and so on, and also financial management, if able.
